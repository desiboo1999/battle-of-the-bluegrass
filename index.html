What Is It?

Battle of the Bluegrass is a voluntary three-month division-based health challenge built around healthy lifestyle improvements, accountability and teamwork.

01

Stay Accountable

Weekly check-ins help participants stay engaged throughout the challenge.

02

Track Progress

Body composition progress is tracked throughout the three-month challenge.

Compete as a Team

Work groups compete by division while working toward healthier outcomes together.

Program Details

Challenge Includes

  • 3 Month Challenge
  • $100 Per Participant
  • Weekly Check-ins
  • Body Composition Tracking
  • Team Competition

Scoring

Scoring is based on average inches lost per division, body fat reduction, muscle gain, and overall body composition improvement.
